# DRAIS v1.90.0 — Explanation Engine + Founder Independence (Phases 9 & 10)

The Attendance Intelligence Program's final two phases, shipped together — closing the program.

## Phase 9 — Explanation Engine
Every attendance decision now explains itself. Instead of a bare "Late", a "Why?" affordance on each matched log row opens the full reasoning chain:
- **Arrival** vs the **on-time cutoff**, the **grace** applied (→ late-after time), the exact **difference**, and **departure**
- The **deciding policy** — named rule, staff shift, or day-specific weekday override, or raw-presence fallback
- A **plain-language reason** ("First check-in at 4:13 PM is after the 8:45 AM late threshold…")

Pure `explainVerdict()` (11 unit tests) + `/api/attendance/explain`. Live on a real JIPRA late verdict it produced a human-readable explanation end to end.

## Phase 10 — Founder Independence Layer
The capstone before/after report at **/attendance/founder-independence** (sidebar): every attendance workflow that once required the founder, its Phase-0 baseline autonomy, what it is now, and the DRAIS surface that handles it — with live per-school signals. Weighted score: **baseline 3% → now 91% (+88)**.

Pure `scoreIndependence()` + `WORKFLOWS` map grounded in the Phase-0 audit (§8), with a test asserting no workflow ever regressed. `/api/attendance/founder-independence`.

## Program complete
Phases 0–10 delivered. A trained administrator can now diagnose, explain, recover and maintain attendance from inside DRAIS with minimal reliance on the founder — the stated final objective.
