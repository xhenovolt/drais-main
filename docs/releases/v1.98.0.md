# DRAIS v1.98.0 — Proactive Attendance Digest (Founder-Independence Phase D)

Closes the systemic "attention" gap from the attendance founder-independence audit: all the intelligence was pull/banner — it waited to be looked at. Now DRAIS tells you.

## What shipped
- **Daily digest**: once a day, DRAIS composes a short, prioritised summary of what attendance needs — gaps, clock drift, identity issues, devices needing maintenance, people/roster to review — and delivers it as an **in-app notification** (cost-free, always available) to each school's admins. Turns "DRAIS notices" into "DRAIS tells you", so nobody has to open a page to catch a dying device.
- **Per-school mode** (Health Center → Daily attendance digest): *Only when there are issues* (default) · *Every day* · *Off*. Plus **Send now** for an on-demand digest and a live preview of what would be sent.
- Delivered via the existing daily cron (after the intelligence sweep populates fresh data), deduped once per school per day, targeted at admin/headteacher/director roles.

## Architecture
- `src/lib/attendance/digest.ts` — **pure** `buildAttendanceDigest()` (9 unit tests: priority escalation, singular/plural, null-safety) + `sendDailyDigests()` orchestration reusing `NotificationService` and the intelligence-summary aggregation.
- `/api/attendance/digest` (GET mode+preview, POST set-mode/send). Cron hook in `/api/result-deadlines`.
