# v1.102.0 — Bilingual corrective hubs (Founder-Independence Phase F, part 1)

**Nature:** minor (a missing part — Arabic + accessibility — added to shipped pages).

## What this closes

Audit gap **FD-6: the intelligence pages were English-only.** A school operating
in Arabic could see the attendance *numbers* but the founder-independence
*controls* — the pages where an administrator actually fixes things — spoke only
English. This ships the first two corrective hubs in full EN + AR, with
accessibility touches, per the standing bilingual policy (EN stays the fallback;
never Arabic-only).

## Localized this release

- **Recovery Center** (`/attendance/recovery`) — every string, including the new
  **Historical repair** panel (Phase E) and the queue/staging recoverables.
- **Identity Intelligence** (`/attendance/identity-intelligence`) — health
  banner, issue counts, and the full **duplicate-people merge** flow (Phase B):
  keeper selection, preview, confirm dialog, and result toasts.

All strings route through `t('attendanceIntel.…', vars, 'English fallback')`.
Interpolated counts/dates use `{{var}}` placeholders so Arabic word order is
correct rather than string-concatenated.

## Accessibility

- Icon-bearing action buttons (Re-scan) carry `aria-label`.
- The Historical-repair date inputs and role selector carry `aria-label` so they
  are announced even when the visible label is terse.

## Under the hood

- New `attendanceIntel` namespace in both `src/locales/en.json` and
  `src/locales/ar.json` — **51 keys, full EN/AR parity** (verified by a walk of
  both trees).
- Sub-components (`HistoricalRepair`, `DuplicatePeople`, `DupGroup`) receive `t`
  by prop so they localize without each opening its own context.

## Remaining in Phase F

Health Center, Device Intelligence, Trends, and Time-Health pages are still
English-only and will follow in a subsequent increment. Phase G (route
robustness / `withRoute`) is unaffected.
