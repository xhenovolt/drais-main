# v1.103.0 — Bilingual intelligence pages, complete (Founder-Independence Phase F, part 2)

**Nature:** minor (the remaining missing part — Arabic + accessibility — added to the
intelligence surface).

## What this closes

Finishes audit gap **FD-6**. With v1.102.0 the two corrective *hubs* went
bilingual; this ships the remaining four intelligence pages in full EN + AR, so
an Arabic-operating school reads the entire attendance-intelligence surface in
its own language — not just the numbers, but every diagnosis, recommendation,
and correction control.

## Localized this release

- **Attendance Health Center** (`/attendance/health`) — pipeline check cards,
  overall verdict, recommendations, and the **Daily digest** control (Phase D):
  mode selector, send-now, and preview line.
- **Device Intelligence** (`/attendance/device-intelligence`) — fleet score,
  per-device reputation sub-bars (Clock / Upload / Heartbeat / Activity), and
  maintenance recommendations.
- **Attendance Trends** (`/attendance/trends`) — trend headline (with a
  translated *improving / declining / stable* word rather than a concatenated
  English token), day-range selector, chart heading, and alerts.
- **Attendance Time Health** (`/attendance/time-health`) — device time-confidence
  verdicts, the ten clock-cause labels, the full **assisted-correction modal**
  (question → preview → apply), and the undoable correction history.

## Notes on quality

- All strings route through `t('attendanceIntel.…', vars, 'English fallback')`;
  counts, times, directions, and shift descriptions are interpolated via
  `{{var}}` so Arabic reads naturally instead of being string-glued.
- The `attendanceIntel` namespace now holds **149 keys with full EN/AR parity**,
  verified two ways: a key-tree walk of both dictionaries, and a scan confirming
  every `attendanceIntel.*` referenced across all six pages resolves in both
  languages.

## Accessibility

- `aria-label` on every icon-only / terse control across the four pages
  (Re-check, digest mode select, day-range select, the correction time input).

## Founder-independence status

Phase F is **complete** — the entire attendance-intelligence surface is bilingual
and EN stays the fallback (never Arabic-only). Remaining: **Phase G** (route
robustness / `withRoute` wrapper), which ties into the route-hardening plan.
